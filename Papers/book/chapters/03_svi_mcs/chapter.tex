\chaptersynopsis{Chapter~2 built the law first and read the smile off it.
This chapter works the other way, directly in the volatility chart, as
the industry does.  The gain is legibility: SVI draws one expiry as a
tilted hyperbola whose jump-wing reading is the desk's vocabulary, and
the Multi-Core Sigmoid adds local body detail by superposing kernels
that are provably silent in both tails.  The price is that validity must
now be policed.  The wing slopes are a moment reading capped strictly
inside Lee's bound --- the bound itself admits negative tail density ---
while the interior escapes every wing condition, as a classical
counterexample shows, and is decided by a dense-grid check instead.  The
jump-wing coordinates invert exactly on a proved domain and lose exactly
the interior curvature on its singular stratum.  A structural
optimization chart makes the enforced inequalities hold at every iterate
by construction.  The chapter closes with the three families of this
book fitted to the same frozen quotes under one protocol: fit quality,
validity margin, and tail behavior, with no family winning every
column.}
