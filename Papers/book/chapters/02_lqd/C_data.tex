\section{Data and reproducibility}
\label{app:data}

Every empirical number and inherited market figure in this alternative chapter
comes from the frozen JSON snapshot used by the book.  It contains SPY and
NVDA option chains for eight expiries each from the 2026-08-03 US session,
together with forwards, discount factors, retained quotes, fitted coefficients,
and display curves.  Reproduction requires no live market-data connection.

The stored fits use $\alpha_-=\alpha_+=0$.  They are rebuilt from the frozen
coefficients rather than refitted, so their prices and all macros remain those
of the published snapshot.  They were fitted one expiry at a time, before the
joint full-line calendar solve of \cref{sec:globalcalendarfit}; the synthetic
stack of \cref{fig:calendarglobal}, not the frozen surface, illustrates that
solve.  The two new figures in this version are synthetic:
\cref{fig:tailfamily} plots the derived asymptotic rates, and
\cref{fig:calendarglobal} plots an exactly specified constant-speed LQD
maturity stack.  Neither introduces market observations.

Tail-policy comparisons recalibrate every exponent scenario under the same
quotes and joint calendar constraints.  Changing $\alpha_\pm$ at fixed body
coefficients is only a local sensitivity.
