\section{The accountant: what the joint solve refuses to invent}
\label{sec:graccount}

An operator that fills a whole universe from a handful of numbers had
better not manufacture information.  This section stress-tests
\cref{eq:grjoint} against the three classic ways a propagation scheme
can cheat: paying attention to a neighbour that knows nothing,
counting one source several times because several routes carry it, and
inventing signal where no path to data exists.  Each test has a sharp
answer, and one of them will force a genuine design decision.

\subsection{The silent neighbour}

Set up the smallest troubling case.  A receiver trusts two informers
at equal precision: one is lit; the other is \emph{dead} --- a dark
dead-end with no observation and no other relations, this morning's
equivalent of an unplugged microphone.  What should the lit message
transfer?

Everything.  The dead informer knows nothing, so configuring trust in
it should cost nothing.  And in the objective \cref{eq:grjoint} that
is automatic, for a reason worth displaying.  First recall the tool:
eliminating a variable from a quadratic --- minimizing it out ---
replaces the remaining variables' information block by its
\emph{Schur complement}: the receiver's own entry, minus (its
coupling to the eliminated variable) times (the inverse of that
variable's entry) times (the coupling back) --- the matrix version of
completing the square.  Now apply it.  The dead informer
$\Theta_D$ appears in exactly one term,
$p\,(\Theta_R-\Theta_D)^{2}$, whose information block on the pair
$(\Theta_R,\Theta_D)$ is
\[
p\begin{pmatrix}1&-1\\-1&1\end{pmatrix};
\qquad\text{eliminating } \Theta_D \text{ leaves }
\Theta_R \text{'s entry at}\quad
p-p\cdot\frac{1}{p}\cdot p=0 .
\]
The complement is exactly zero: a factor
whose informer is otherwise free constrains the receiver \emph{not at
all}.  The dead branch evaporates, the live factor
$p(\Theta_R-\Theta_A)^{2}$ is untouched, and the receiver takes the
whole message.  \Cref{fig:graccount}(a): across four decades of
configured dead-informer trust, the transfer is
\MacGrDeadPairMin\ --- flat, at unity.

Now the design decision the test exposes.  There is a natural-looking
alternative way to assemble the same beliefs: instead of one square
per relation, charge each receiver \emph{once} for deviating from its
precision-weighted forecast \cref{eq:grcond} --- a single row
$q\,(\Theta_R-\tfrac12\Theta_A-\tfrac12\Theta_D)^{2}$ with
$q=2p$, in our equal-trust case.  Locally the two assemblies are
indistinguishable: both produce exactly the receiver conditional
\cref{eq:grcond}, so no one-node test can tell them apart.  But they
differ as \emph{joint} distributions, and the dead informer finds the
difference.  The averaging row's information matrix is the rank-one
form $q\,cc^{\!\top}$ with $c=(1,-\tfrac12,-\tfrac12)$ on
$(\Theta_R,\Theta_A,\Theta_D)$, and eliminating $\Theta_D$ from it
annihilates the \emph{entire row}:
\[
q\begin{pmatrix}1&-\tfrac12\\[1pt]-\tfrac12&\tfrac14\end{pmatrix}
-\frac{1}{q/4}\,
q^{2}\begin{pmatrix}\tfrac14&-\tfrac18\\[1pt]-\tfrac18&\tfrac1{16}
\end{pmatrix}
=0 .
\]
The dead informer sat inside the same square as the live message, so
removing it removed the receiver's tie to the live informer too.  The
posterior is left \emph{improper} --- flat along a direction through
the live signal --- and any small regularization then picks an
arbitrary point on the flat.  Concretely, add a vanishing ridge
$\varepsilon\,\Theta^{2}$ to every node: the ridge resolves the flat
direction at its smallest-norm point.  At equal trust, with the live
informer at the unit message, the flat is
$\Theta_R=\tfrac12+\tfrac12\Theta_D$, and minimizing
$\Theta_R^{2}+\Theta_D^{2}$ along it gives $\Theta_D=-\tfrac15$,
$\Theta_R=\MacGrDeadAvgEq$ --- forty percent of the message, from a
tie-break the desk never chose, decaying to
\MacGrDeadAvgHigh\ as the silent neighbour's configured trust grows
(the violet curve of \cref{fig:graccount}(a)).  Configuring trust in
a neighbour that says nothing \emph{destroys} a live signal.

The lesson generalizes well beyond this example, so state it plainly:
\emph{the joint form of an operator is a modelling decision, even when
every local conditional agrees}
\citep{Koller2009,Pearl1988}.  The book's assembly is therefore one
factor per relation --- which is also why a relation entered in both
directions must be recognized as \emph{one} fact (the two readings of
\cref{sec:gredge}), not two facts whose precisions add.

\subsection{One source, two routes}

The second cheat is subtler.  Let one source $S$ be observed --- at
\emph{finite} precision $p$, so its own value is uncertain --- and let
its message reach a target $T$ twice: directly, and through a middle
node $M$.  All three relations carry precision $p$ and amplitude one.
Intuition from \cref{eq:grchain} prices each route as a message: the
direct route delivers the source's value with variance
$1/p+1/p=2/p$ (the source's own doubt plus one relation's noise); the
two-leg route delivers it with variance $3/p$.  Two messages, so add
their precisions: $p/2+p/3=5p/6$, a claimed variance of
$6/(5p)=\MacGrRepeatNaive/p$ at the target.

The joint solve disagrees.  Assemble \cref{eq:grjoint} for the
triangle --- ordering the nodes $(S,M,T)$, the observation contributes
$p$ to the top-left entry and each relation its $2\times2$ block:
\[
\mathcal{I}^{+}
=p\begin{pmatrix}3&-1&-1\\-1&2&-1\\-1&-1&2\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
\det=3p^{3},
\qquad
\mathcal{V}^{+}_T
=\frac{p^{2}\det\begin{pmatrix}3&-1\\-1&2\end{pmatrix}}{3p^{3}}
=\frac{5}{3p},
\]
the middle expression being the cofactor rule for one diagonal entry
of the inverse.  The honest variance is
$\MacGrRepeatJoint/p$ --- not $\MacGrRepeatNaive/p$.  Per-route
accounting claims precision $5p/6$ where the truth is $3p/5$: an
overstatement of \MacGrRepeatOverstatePct\% of the honest figure, and
the error has a name: the two routes
are not independent witnesses, because both carry the \emph{same}
source's doubt.  However many wires fan out of one uncertain node,
that node's own variance crosses every one of them; it can never be
diversified away by adding routes.  The joint solve keeps the
covariance the per-route bookkeeping forgot.
\Cref{fig:graccount}(b) shows the two claims side by side.

The practical consequence is a rule for \emph{attribution}.  The
posterior mean is linear in the observations --- solving the normal
equations, $\hat\Theta^{+}_i=\sum_s
\mathcal{K}_{is}\,\hat\Theta_{\mathrm{obs},s}$ plus the
residual-offset terms of \cref{sec:grbelieve} (the anchors, aiming at
zero, shape the matrix but add no term), where $\mathcal{K}_{is}$ is
the derivative of node $i$'s posterior with respect to lit node $s$'s
measurement.  This matrix is Chapter~10's gain
$\mathcal{K}$ grown to universe size, and it decomposes every filled
node over the lit \emph{sources}, exactly and additively.  Ask
``which routes carried it?''\ and the question has no well-posed
answer --- route contributions overlap, as the triangle just showed.
Ask ``which sources earned it?''\ and the answer is a row of
$\mathcal{K}$.  Attribution is by source, never by path.

\subsection{No path, no signal}

The last test needs no algebra.  Take a component of the graph ---
some cluster of nodes connected to each other but to no lit node.
Restricted to it, the objective \cref{eq:grjoint} sees contracts only:
every term is a difference, so adding one constant to the whole
cluster changes nothing --- the objective is flat, the information
matrix singular, the posterior improper.  The machinery must
\emph{refuse}: no solve, no invented number.  The honest publication
for such a component is the one \cref{sec:grdark} already named ---
each node's baseline, unchanged ($\Theta=0$), under a stated, wide
band (one typical morning's move is the reference implementation's
convention), with a flag saying \emph{no lit path} rather than a
number pretending to be inference.  Silence honestly labelled is a
feature; the alternative --- regularizing the singularity away and
publishing the result --- is the averaging assembly's mistake wearing
a different hat.

The accounting holds: silent neighbours cost nothing, sources are
counted once, absence is labelled absence.  What the contracts still
lack is any way to say the one thing every desk eventually asks for
--- ``believe the relation, but not at full force.''  That dial is
next.
