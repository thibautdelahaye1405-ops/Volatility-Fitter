\section{The construction protocol}
\label{app:grprotocol}

Every number in this chapter regenerates from one deterministic script
reading the frozen snapshot; this appendix records the recipe.

\paragraph{The universe.}
Twelve real nodes: the frozen SPY and NVDA boards at the six expiries
of 18~days and beyond (variance times $0.049$ to $1.373$); the two
sub-week expiries are excluded because maturity-ratio amplitudes
across day-scale gaps are statements about Chapter~8's event clock,
which this chapter does not model.  Each real node's true handle level
is the ATM volatility of the snapshot's stored haircut fit --- the
same stored smiles Chapter~9 transported.  Four synthetic nodes per
synthetic name, at the four middle expiries: the \emph{sister}'s true
level is NVDA's minus $2.0$ points; the \emph{blend}'s is the
SPY/NVDA average plus $0.5$.  Twenty nodes in all.

\paragraph{The staged morning.}
The true innovation field is
\[
\Theta^{*}_{(\text{name},T)}
=b_{\text{name}}\cdot
\MacGrAnchorMovePts\cdot\frac{\tau_{\text{Dec}}}{\tau_T}
\;+\;\varepsilon_{(\text{name},T)}
\;+\;\mathcal{U}\cdot\1_{\{\text{name}=\text{sister}\}},
\]
with betas $b=(1,\MacGrBetaNvda,\MacGrBetaSister,\MacGrBetaBlend)$
for (SPY, NVDA, sister, blend) --- so the asserted
sister${\leftarrow}$NVDA edge at $\beta=0.9$ is exactly consistent,
$0.9\times1.6=1.44$ --- i.i.d.\ scatter
$\varepsilon\sim\mathcal{N}(0,\MacGrScatterSd^{2})$ from one seeded
draw (seed 11711 --- the chapter's only randomness), and the sister's
carried dislocation $\mathcal{U}=\MacGrSisterCarried$: a $-0.90$-point
dislocation measured at a print $0.75$~days ago, decayed by
\cref{eq:grdecay} at the stated half-life $t_{1/2}=2$~days.
Yesterday's handle at every node is defined as truth minus
$\Theta^{*}$; the constructed overnight moves no forwards, so
Chapter~9's transport is the identity and the baselines are exact
(the $\mathcal{V}_{\mathrm{base}}$ term of
\cref{sec:grmeet}'s observation rule is zero in this construction; on
a live desk it is not).

\paragraph{What the solver sees.}
The baselines; the eight lit measurements
$\hat\Theta_{\mathrm{obs},s}=\Theta^{*}_s+
\mathcal{N}(0,\MacGrObsSd^{2})$ (a second seeded draw) at the six SPY
nodes and the NVDA 46- and 228-day nodes, with
$\mathcal{V}_{\mathrm{obs}}=\MacGrObsSd^{2}$; and the contracts.
Calendar edges join adjacent expiries within each name, quoted with
the shorter maturity as receiver at the amplitude
\cref{eq:grcalbeta}; cross edges join NVDA${\leftarrow}$SPY
($\beta=\MacGrBetaNvda$), sister${\leftarrow}$NVDA ($\beta=0.9$,
carrying the residual offset of \cref{sec:grbelieve}, with the
residual's growth since the print --- standard deviation
$0.20$~points --- widening the stated relation noise) and
blend${\leftarrow}$SPY
($\beta=\MacGrBetaBlend$) at shared expiries.  Every stated relation
variance is the construction's own scatter propagated through the
edge, $\MacGrScatterSd^{2}(1+\beta_{ij}^{2})$.  The anchors
$\kappa_i$ are zero: in this constructed world the relations
genuinely transfer at full force, so a shrink would misstate the
world --- a live desk, less sure of its relations, would set
$\kappa>0$ as \cref{sec:granchor} prescribes.  The overtrusting audit
arm multiplies every relation precision by $25$.

\paragraph{The solve and the outputs.}
The posterior minimizes \cref{eq:grjoint} exactly as displayed ---
the information matrix is assembled term by term, the normal
equations are solved densely, marginal variances are the diagonal of
the inverse, and the attribution $\mathcal{K}_{is}$ is the
derivative of the posterior in each lit measurement.  The
dark-node errors, the skill scatter, the audit statistics and the
decomposition numbers of \cref{sec:grcomplete} all read off this one
solve (plus the no-residual and overtrusted variants).  The withheld
NVDA December reconstruction applies the posterior level innovation
to the baseline smile and retargets by refitting the Chapter-2 family
(order 16, the ridge of Chapter~3's protocol) to the shifted curve on
the node's own strike grid; the quoted \MacGrRetargetRmsBp-basis-point
rms is the gap between the refit and the shifted target.  The
figure's band is the floored marginal,
$\pm\sqrt{\smash[b]{\mathcal{V}^{+}+\MacGrScatterSd^{2}}}$.

\paragraph{The toy exhibits.}
The ladder of \cref{fig:grcontract,fig:grmeet}, the dead-informer and
triangle fixtures of \cref{fig:graccount}, the corroboration sweep of
\cref{fig:granchor} and the asynchronous pair of \cref{fig:grstory}
are pure synthetic constructions with the parameters printed in the
text; each runs through the same solver as the universe (the
averaging assembly of \cref{sec:graccount} is implemented once,
with a $10^{-8}$ ridge, for the foil).  No fixture number is typed by
hand: every value the text quotes is emitted by the figure script as
a macro.
