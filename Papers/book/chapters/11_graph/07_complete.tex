\section{The universe completed}
\label{sec:grcomplete}

Now run everything at once on the universe of
\cref{fig:gruniverse}.  The experiment is constructed \emph{backward}
from the frozen data, so that the truth at every dark real node is the
actual frozen board.  The construction has three ingredients.  A
systematic repricing: the index's ATM volatility moves
\MacGrAnchorMovePts\ points at its December node, scaled across
maturities by the constant-variance-injection shape of
\cref{sec:gredge} and per name by its stated beta (\MacGrBetaNvda\
for NVDA, \MacGrBetaSister\ for the sister, \MacGrBetaBlend\ for the
blend).  A seeded idiosyncratic scatter of \MacGrScatterSd\ points at
every node --- each node's private move.  And the sister's carried
dislocation, \MacGrSisterCarried\ points this morning.  The three
together are the true innovation field; yesterday is then
\emph{defined} as today's truth minus that field.  The solver is
shown only yesterday, the \MacGrLitCount\ lit measurements (truth
plus \MacGrObsSd\ points of stated observation noise), and the
contracts, whose stated relation noise is the construction's own
scatter, propagated.  Every number is deterministic given the one
seeded draw; Appendix~\ref{app:grprotocol} lists the recipe.  The
\MacGrDarkCount\ dark nodes are the held-out truths.

\subsection{Reading the completed board}

Start with the aggregate: across the twelve dark nodes, riding the
baselines --- publishing yesterday unchanged --- errs by
\MacGrRmsBase\ volatility points rms; the graph posterior errs by
\MacGrRmsGraph.  The gap is this morning's systematic content, and the
individual nodes in \cref{fig:grcomplete}(a) show where it lives.
Each dot is one dark node, its baseline error on the horizontal axis
and its graph error on the vertical; the dotted diagonal is ``no
better than riding the prior.''  \MacGrSkillBetter\ of the twelve sit
below the diagonal --- most far below.  The extreme case is the dark
18-day NVDA node: its true innovation was \MacGrShortDarkTrue\ points
(the short end of a repricing morning), the baseline missed by all of
it, and the graph --- hearing the lit index board and NVDA's own lit
neighbours --- reconstructed \MacGrShortDarkPost.  The
\MacGrSkillWorse\ dots at or above the diagonal are both sister
nodes whose mornings were dominated by their own scatter; one sits
essentially on the line, and the called-out one is the extreme:
truth barely moved (baseline error
\MacGrCalmBase\ points) while its private draw fought the systematic,
and the graph, correctly reporting the systematic, missed by
\MacGrCalmGraph.  Those dots are the mechanism's honest scope printed
on one chart: the graph carries \emph{shared} repricing; it cannot
manufacture a node's private move, and on a morning that is all
private moves its honest answer degrades to ``no change'' --- which is
the baseline, not a disaster.

Panel~(b) is the chapter's --- and the book's --- closing exhibit.  The
NVDA December node is dark this morning; its truth is the real frozen
board --- \MacGrNvdaDecQuotes\ prepared quotes the solver never saw.  The dashed
green curve is its baseline: yesterday's smile, which this morning's
repricing has left \MacGrNvdaDecTrue\ points too low.  The blue curve
is the reconstruction: the posterior innovation
$\hat\Theta^{+}=\MacGrNvdaDecPost$ points --- earned, per the
attribution rule of \cref{sec:graccount}, as
\MacGrNvdaDecFromNvda\ from the two lit NVDA neighbours,
\MacGrNvdaDecFromSpy\ from the six lit index nodes, and
\MacGrNvdaDecFromRes\ routed through the sister's carried residual ---
applied to the baseline and retargeted through Part~I's machinery
into an arbitrage-free smile (the retarget reproduces the shifted
target to \MacGrRetargetRmsBp\ volatility basis points rms, so
validity costs essentially nothing).  The orange dots are the withheld
quotes.  The reconstruction lands within a fraction of a point of a
smile it never saw, and --- the part that matters as much --- its
shaded $\pm\MacGrNvdaDecBand$-point band \emph{contains} the miss.
The true innovation was \MacGrNvdaDecTrue; the posterior said
\MacGrNvdaDecPost\ and owned the difference.

The sister board shows the third prior doing its work.  At the dark
sister September node, solving the same morning \emph{without} the
carried dislocation publishes $\MacGrSisterSepSys$ points --- the
systematic message alone.  With the residual carried
(\MacGrSisterCarried\ at the morning's decay), the published mark is
$\MacGrSisterSepPost$, against a truth of \MacGrSisterSepTrue.  The
residual channel moved the mark by roughly eight tenths of a point
--- slightly more than the carried value itself, because the
dislocation also echoes back through the sister's own ladder --- and
it moved it toward the truth.  Yesterday afternoon's one thin print is still
paying for marks this morning, at a stated, decaying weight, not
forever.

\subsection{The audit, and one honest repair}

Chapter~10 ended by demanding that any machinery publishing
uncertainty be audited: the standardized errors
$\mathcal{Z}=(\text{truth}-\hat\Theta^{+})/\sqrt{\mathcal{V}}$ of
\cref{eq:infz} should scatter with standard deviation one.
Panel~(c) runs that audit over the twelve dark nodes, three ways.
First, an \emph{overtrusting} arm --- every relation precision
multiplied by twenty-five, the configuration mistake of a desk that
states conviction it does not have: std$(\mathcal{Z})
=\MacGrAuditOver$, bars several times too narrow, Chapter~10's
starved-filter failure in graph clothes.  Second, the \emph{stated}
precisions: \MacGrAuditStated\ --- better, but still above one, and
the excess is diagnosable.  The edges into one receiver were priced
as if their noises were independent, but every one of them shares the
\emph{receiver's own} idiosyncratic scatter; adding their precisions
therefore over-credits the neighbourhood, by exactly the mechanism of
the repeated-route case file (\cref{sec:graccount}) acting through
the node itself.  Third, the repair: a node's neighbours can never
know its private scatter, so the published band can never honestly be
narrower than it --- add the node's own scatter variance to the band
(the \emph{idiosyncratic floor}; it touches no mean, only bands).
The audit lands at \MacGrAuditFloor.  A twelve-residual audit is a
coarse instrument, but the ordering it prints is the lesson:
overconfidence is a configuration property, honesty is checkable, and
the checks are the same ones Chapter~10 taught --- run at universe
scale.

Hold the chapter against the book's thesis one last time.
\emph{Validity} is inherited by construction: the graph moves handles,
and every published smile is retargeted through Part~I's machinery ---
the universe never contains a curve that fails its own chapter-2
checks.  \emph{Information} is the chapter's whole subject, made
geographic: the lit innovations are the morning's entire evidence, and
the machinery's discipline is a list of refusals --- to move without
being paid, to hear a silent neighbour, to count one source twice, to
report conditional confidence as marginal, to invent signal where no
path exists.  \emph{Convention} is everything else, and every piece of
it is a stated, auditable number: the amplitudes, the relation
precisions, the anchor, the half-life, the floor.  Posterior equals
data where data speaks; structure where data is silent; and
uncertainty always, audited.

\subsection{The end of the book}

Close the loop.  This book opened with a predicament: a market that
quotes finitely many noisy prices, and a desk that must nevertheless
publish something as ambitious as a \emph{field of probability laws}
--- a law for every underlier, every expiry, every strike, every
morning.  The chapters built the answer in layers.  A single smile
became a probability law with checkable validity (Part~I).  The
observation itself was earned --- forwards, exercise rights, the
market's own clock (Part~II).  The surface was given dynamics, and
inference under weak information --- the filter, the prior, the gate
--- taught one discipline above all: say what the data determined,
say what convention filled in, and price the difference
(Part~III).  This chapter assembled all of it into the object the
predicament actually demands: not one smile but a universe of them,
sparse data and all, each law published with its provenance --- this
much from today's quotes, this much from the structure the desk
asserted and disclosed, this much from a remembered dislocation on a
stated clock, and a band, audited, over all of it.  That a
twenty-node universe with eight lit nodes can hand back a withheld
December smile to within a fraction of a volatility point is not a
conjuring trick; it is bookkeeping --- Gaussian, checkable, and honest
about every assumption it spends.  The surface was never one object.
It is a field of laws, held together by everything a desk knows and
everything it is willing to say --- and the whole of this book has
been the discipline of keeping those two accounts separate, and
adding them up correctly.
