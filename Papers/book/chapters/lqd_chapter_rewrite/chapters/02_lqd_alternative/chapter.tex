\chapter[The log-quantile-density model]{The Log-Quantile-Density Model of
the Volatility Smile}
\label{ch:lqd}

\chaptersynopsis{A volatility smile is a probability law viewed through the
Black formula.  The log-quantile-density model builds the law directly: start
from percentile rank, move through log-return at a strictly positive speed,
then shift the result to match the forward.  Two tail exponents span the range
from exponential to Gaussian-rate log-return tails.  One cumulative
upper-share function then supplies prices, digitals, density, local smile
derivatives, moments, calibration sensitivities, and calendar constraints.
Across expiries, ordering that function on the whole rank line is exactly
equivalent to noncrossing total-variance curves on the whole strike line.}
