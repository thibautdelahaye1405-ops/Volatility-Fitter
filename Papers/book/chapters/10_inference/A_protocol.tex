\section{Protocol: data and constants}
\label{app:infprotocol}

Every number and figure in this chapter regenerates from one
deterministic script reading the frozen snapshot
(\texttt{scripts/ch10/gen\_figures.py}); nothing is refetched and no
stored fit is altered.  The audit figure's seeded walk is the
chapter's only randomness.

\paragraph{The staged morning (\cref{fig:infflat,fig:infbasket}).}
The real-data exhibit is the book's canonical SPY December 2026 node
(\MacPriorFlatHeroDays\ days, \MacPriorFlatNFull\ prepared quotes),
thinned to the \MacPriorFlatNThin\ quotes with
$\lvert k\rvert\le0.10$.  Each quote's stated noise is its bid--ask
half-spread in volatility, floored at one vol bp.

\paragraph{The spline family
(\cref{fig:infflat,fig:infjump}).}
The deliberately flexible family is a natural cubic spline in $k$
--- linear in its knot values, so every least-squares statement in
the chapter is exact rather than linearized.  Its knots sit at
\[
k\in\{-0.36,-0.30,-0.22,-0.15,-0.09,-0.04,0,0.04,0.09,0.15,0.26\}
\]
for the profile experiment and at
\[
k\in\{-0.30,-0.20,-0.12,-0.05,0,0.05,0.12,0.20,0.30\}
\]
for the overnight experiment.  A mild second-difference penalty on
the knot values (weights $0.5$ and $3.0$ respectively, in whitened
units) keeps the unquoted knots finite; it is the ``smoothness
rule'' named in the text.

\paragraph{The pinned-wing profile (\cref{fig:infflat}).}
For each imposed value the fitted volatility at $k=-0.30$ is
constrained by one row at effectively infinite weight ($10^{12}$)
and the family is refitted; the plotted statistic is the plain rms
of (fit $-$ mid) over the fitted quote set, in vol bp.  The sweep
runs \MacPriorFlatPinLoPct--\MacPriorFlatPinHiPct\% in 25 steps.
The ensemble sentence in \cref{sec:infflat} refits the thinned band
with the reference implementation under Chapter~3's protocol: one
family at three resolutions and two ridge strengths, plus the two
other parametric families --- eight fits, wing readings compared at
$k=-0.30$.

\paragraph{Support and gates
(\cref{eq:infsupport,eq:infgate}; \cref{fig:infgate,fig:infbasket}).}
Kernel bandwidth $0.06$ in log-moneyness throughout; fit weights
normalized to average one; required precision
$\mathcal{I}_{\mathrm{req}}=1$ (``one effective quote''); gate
exponent $\gamma=1$ except where \cref{fig:infgate}(a) varies it.
\Cref{fig:infbasket}(a) spreads nine equally-weighted quotes
uniformly over $[-W,W]$ and sweeps the half-width $W$; the wing legs
sit at $\pm\MacPriorBasketLeg$.

\paragraph{The overnight experiment (\cref{fig:infjump}).}
Yesterday's smile is $0.20-0.35k+0.80k^2$; today's truth adds
\MacPriorJumpPts\ points uniformly.  Today's quotes: 15 mids of the
truth at equal spacing on $[-0.10,0.10]$, stated noise 30~bp.  Shape
baskets: five candidate rows (level; risk reversal and butterfly at
leg pairs $\pm0.20$ and $\pm0.30$), targets evaluated on yesterday's
curve; each row is priced by
\cref{eq:infharmonic}, gated by \cref{eq:infgate}
($\mathcal{I}_{\mathrm{req}}=1$, $\gamma=1$), and a total budget
worth twenty clean quotes is split over the open gates in proportion
to their values --- the closed level and moderate-butterfly rows
(gates \MacPriorJumpGateAtm\ and \MacPriorJumpGateBfMod) are
dropped.  Absolute anchors: yesterday's volatilities imposed at the
four wing strikes, the same total budget split equally.  Errors are
read at $k=-0.30$.

\paragraph{The observation budget (\cref{fig:infcovar}).}
A synthetic chain of \MacFiltCovarNq\ quotes at equal spacing on
$[-0.25,0.25]$, truth $0.20-0.30k+0.60k^2$, base noise 30~bp,
fitted by the quadratic family $\theta_0+\theta_1k+\theta_2k^2$
whose parameters are the handles (curvature $=2\theta_2$), so
\cref{eq:infinfo,eq:infdelta} are exact.  The realized-misfit
multiple is
$\max\bigl(1,\ \chi^2/(m-d)\bigr)$ capped at $25$, with $\chi^2$
the sum of squared whitened quote residuals, $m$ the quote count
and $d$ the parameter count.  Panel~(b) kinks the two adjacent
strikes nearest $k=+0.03$ by $\pm\epsilon$ with $\epsilon$ swept to
three vol points, and evaluates gains against a stated prediction
budget of $(60\,\text{bp},\ 0.03,\ 0.15)$ per handle sd --- the
level entry is a four-day-old state under the walk scale of
\cref{fig:infaudit} ($\sqrt{4}\times30$~bp).

\paragraph{The stale-strike vignette (\cref{fig:infupdate}).}
Stated moments as printed in \cref{sec:inffilter}; the update is
\cref{eq:infupdate} verbatim, per handle.

\paragraph{The audit world (\cref{fig:infaudit}).}
Seed $20260807$ (NumPy default generator).  Truth: level starts at
$20\%$ and takes \MacFiltAuditDays\ daily Gaussian steps of sd
\MacFiltAuditQTrueBp\ bp, plus $+\MacFiltAuditShockPts$-point jumps
after days 151 and 320.  Observations: truth plus Gaussian noise of
sd 30~bp, except every seventh (``wide-spread'') day at 100~bp ---
neither jump day is a wide day --- and the filter is always told the
true per-day noise.  Filters: \cref{eq:infupdate} with
$\mathcal{V}^{-}=\mathcal{V}^{+}_{\mathrm{prev}}+q_{\mathrm{walk}}^2$
per day; the starved run uses $q_{\mathrm{walk}}=10$~bp, the honest
run \MacFiltAuditQTrueBp~bp; the surprise-widened run multiplies
$\mathcal{V}^{-}$ by $(\text{standardized innovation}/3)^2$, capped
at $25$, when the innovation exceeds $3$ standardized units.
$\mathcal{Z}$ is computed against the known truth from day two on;
panel~(c) averages absolute errors over the two jumps.
